Our Approach to Privacy: Your salary inputs and calculations are designed to be processed locally on your device. However, the application uses Google AdMob advertising services, which may collect certain device, advertising, and usage information. Please read this policy to understand the distinction.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Salary & Paycheck Calculator, developed and published by ORINEXA ("we", "our", or "us").

We respect your privacy and aim to minimize the information processed by our application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our Android application, including its salary calculations, salary comparisons, reports, and advertising features.

2. Information You Enter

The application allows you to enter information such as:

  • Annual CTC and gross salary.
  • Basic salary, allowances, and bonuses.
  • House Rent Allowance (HRA) and rent-related inputs.
  • Employee and employer PF contributions.
  • Tax regime selections and eligible deductions.
  • Job offer and salary comparison information.
  • Other salary and deduction details.

These inputs are used by the application to calculate estimated salary, deductions, and take-home pay. The salary calculation functionality is designed to operate locally on your device without requiring an account or sending your salary inputs to our own server.

3. Local Storage and Processing

The application may store information locally on your device to support its features.

  • Salary calculations: Salary inputs and calculations are processed locally using the application's calculation logic.
  • Saved information: Saved salary profiles, preferences, and job offer comparisons may be stored in the application's private local storage.
  • PDF reports: Reports are generated on your device. If you choose to share a report, it is handled through the Android sharing interface and the application you select.

We do not operate a user account system or our own cloud service for storing your salary calculations. Information stored locally may be deleted if you clear the application's storage or uninstall the application.

4. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Salary & Paycheck Calculator uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and support the free availability of the application.

Google and its advertising services may automatically collect or process certain information, depending on your device, consent choices, advertising configuration, and applicable settings.

This information may include:

  • Android Advertising ID or other device identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Device information and technical identifiers.
  • App interactions and advertising interactions.
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and video engagement.
  • Diagnostics, performance, and fraud-prevention information.

This information may be used by Google and its advertising partners for advertising delivery, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and other purposes described in their privacy policies.

We do not directly receive or store your salary calculation inputs through our own advertising server. However, third-party SDKs operate according to their own data practices.

Learn more about Google's privacy practices: Google Privacy Policy

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

5. Advertising Preferences and Consent

Where required by applicable law, Google consent tools may be used to collect and manage your advertising consent preferences.

You may also be able to manage advertising preferences or reset your advertising identifier through your Android device settings, depending on your device and Android version.

Advertising personalization and data processing are subject to Google's policies, your consent choices, and applicable privacy requirements.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal salary information. The application does not provide our own account-based data-sharing or salary-upload service.

However, certain technical and advertising information may be collected or shared by Google AdMob and its associated service providers as described in Section 4.

When you explicitly share a PDF report using Android's sharing feature, the report is provided to the application or destination you select.

7. Permissions

The application may use the following Android permissions to support its functionality:

  • INTERNET: Required for network-based services, including retrieving advertisements.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check network connectivity where required.

The actual permissions requested by your installed application are determined by its Android manifest and the integrated software development kits.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Salary information saved by the application remains in the application's local storage until it is deleted or the application data is cleared.

Depending on the available features, you can remove saved calculations through the application's reset or clear-data options.

You can also clear the application's storage or uninstall the application through Android settings to remove locally stored application data.

Information independently collected by Google AdMob is subject to Google's data retention and deletion practices.

9. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through our own account system.

Advertising services integrated into the application are subject to their own policies and applicable age-related requirements.

10. Data Security

We aim to minimize the amount of personal information handled by our application. Salary calculations are designed to be processed locally, and saved application data is maintained within the application's local storage environment.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information processed by third-party services or stored on your device.
