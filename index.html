Privacy Policy

Salary & Paycheck Calculator (SalaryWise) • Effective Date: October 26, 2026

🔒 100% Offline Privacy Guarantee: Your salary, CTC breakdown, tax deductions, and financial calculations are processed exclusively on your device. We do not collect, upload, or transmit your private financial data to any remote server.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Salary & Paycheck Calculator ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles information when you use our offline salary, tax, and paycheck calculation tools.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Our app operates on a privacy-first, offline-first architecture:

  • Financial Inputs & Calculations: All CTC figures, basic pay percentages, HRA rent inputs, tax regime selections, and custom deduction entries are processed purely locally on your device using Kotlin calculation logic.
  • Local Database Storage: Saved salary profiles and job offer comparison slots are stored strictly in an encrypted/private local SQLite database (Android Room) on your device. This data never leaves your device.
  • PDF Report Generation: Payslip PDF reports are generated locally in your app's internal cache folder and shared only when you explicitly invoke the system share action.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep the application free for all users, our app integrates Google AdMob for banner and optional rewarded video advertisements:

  • Google AdMob: AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), coarse location, or IP address to serve advertisements in compliance with Google Play Advertising Policies and local regulations.
  • User Consent & Controls: You can enable or disable personalized ads at any time via the Preferences section in the app's Tools tab.

4. Device Permissions

The app requests minimal Android system permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely for retrieving AdMob ad units and verifying consent preferences.

5. Data Control & Reset

You have full control over your locally saved data at all times. You can instantly clear all saved calculations, job offer slots, and app preferences by tapping Reset Defaults or Clear Saved Calculations in the Tools tab.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected within the app's internal Privacy Policy viewer and this published page.
